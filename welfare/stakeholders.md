# SMALL-C — stakeholders

*Owned by WELFARE. Reviewed 2026-10-27. Review again when the project changes shape (a new department, a repo going public, a new collaborator). Added in the retrofit from synergy's template, 2026-10-27.*

*A stakeholder is anyone with a stake in the project. Key stakeholders are the subset whose driver is the project's best interests — "not all stake holders will necessarily have the project's best interests as their driver" (Jeremy, 2026-10-27 17:51). Welfare is for the key stakeholders; the others are listed so the project knows they're there.*
